# C 字母翻译练习

**1.** 盗用知识产权、贩卖公司机密让他失去了工作。(cost)

> Stealing **intellectual property** and selling company's secrets cost him his job.

**2.** 了解一门语言的前提是熟练掌握至少两门语言。（command）

> The **prerequisite** for understanding a language is having a good command of at least two languages.

**3.** 抱怨不过是浪费时间，它只宣泄情绪，却不能解决问题。（complaint）

> Making complaints is just a waste of time, which only **lets off steam / releases emotions**, but can't solve problems.

**4.** 据报道，由于他的行为违反了职业道德，他被董事会开除了。（conduct）

> **It is reported that** he was fired by the **board** because his **conduct** violated professional ethics.

**5.** 一听说她大学的导师要来主持会议，她忍不住高兴地跳了起来。（Hardly, chair）

> **Hardly** had she heard that her **mentor** at university would **chair the meeting** **when** she couldn't help jumping with great joy.

**6.** 你得克制自己的本能，不能随随便便下结论说 Jeff 得为你儿子的死负责。（check）

> You should **keep your instincts in check** instead of jumping to a conclusion that Jeff is responsible for your son's death.

**7.** Maria 良心不安，找神父忏悔，却发现那神父是个乔装打扮的侦探。（confess）

> Maria had a guilty conscience and turned to a priest to **confess her crime**, only to find that the priest was a detective **in disguise**.

**8.** 我们的成功主要应归功于团队协作，包括及时沟通、互相尊重和共同创新。（credit）

> Our success should be mainly credited to **teamwork**, including timely communication, mutual respect and joint innovation.

**9.** Jerry 一被关在笼子里面，就可怜兮兮地叫唤，仿佛自己是天下最孤独的狗，没人疼没人爱。（cage）

> Once Jerry is **caged** / put in the cage, he whines **pitifully**, **as if** he were the loneliest dog in the world, without even the slightest **love or affection**.

**10.** 正是因为他的说辞和警方的调查一致，他从警察局中被放了出来，当晚就回到了自己舒适的小窝。（coincide）

> It was because his statement **coincided with** the police investigation that he was **released** from the police station and returned to his comfortable home that night.

**11.** 你不可能杀了人还能逍遥法外，你会被指控故意伤害罪。（chance, charge）

> There's no chance that you can **get away with it** after you **commit murder**, and instead, you'll be charged with **intentional injury**.

**12.** 《詹森艺术史》这本书我读得津津有味，是因为其中涉及到的艺术品，都是我去年夏天在意大利亲眼见过的。（concern）

> The reason why I **take delight in reading** Janson's History of Art is that I saw all the artworks **concerned** with my own eyes last summer in Italy.

**13.** 一味限制电子书的发行毫无用处，导致上百家实体书店关门的原因其实是人们碎片化的阅读习惯。（claim）

> It's no use limiting the **release** of electronic books, because it is people's fragmented reading habit that has **claimed** hundreds of physical bookshops.
>
> claim 在这里的用法（意为“夺走、导致丧失”，如 The earthquake claimed 100 lives）

**14.** 中国政府致力于消除贫困，有信心在 2020 前全面建成小康社会。（commit）

> The Chinese government **is committed to** eliminating poverty, having confidence in building a **moderately-prosperous society in all respects** by 2020.

**15.** 受到新冠疫情的影响，刘亦菲主演的花木兰很可能无法如期在美国上映。（chance）

> Influenced by the novel coronavirus, chances are that Hua Mulan, **featuring / starring** Liu Yifei, won't be released in America **as scheduled / on schedule**.

**16.** 妇女节那天，张伟丽卫冕 UFC 冠军，为此，人民日报专门发文盛赞它展现了中国女性的力量。（credit）

> On Women's Day, Zhang Weili defended the UFC championship, for which People's Daily specially published an article to **credit her with** showing Chinese women's strength.

**17.** HM 大规模关店，Forever 21 撤出中国，这表明了随着环保意识越来越强，低廉的价格已无法吸引消费者。（check）

> HM is closing its stores on a large scale, and Forever 21 is **checking out of / withdrawing from** China, which shows that with a growing environmental consciousness, low prices can't **appeal to consumers** anymore.

**18.** 不仅在场观众被唐诗逸的舞蹈所倾倒，远在千里之外的网友也用哔哩哔哩发弹幕表达对女神姐姐的欣赏。（carry）

> Not only are the audience present **carried away / fascinated by** Tang Shiyi's dancing, but netizens thousands of miles away also express their admiration for the Goddess by **sending bullet comments** through Bilibili.
