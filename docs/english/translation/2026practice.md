**1.** **坚持**每天锻炼不仅能**增强体质**，还能缓解压力。（relieve）

> **Sticking to** daily exercise can not only **improve physical fitness** but also relieve stress.

**2.** 每每回想起小时候和外婆度过的快乐时光，他都情不自禁地感到温暖。（Every time）

> Every time he recalls the happy moments spent with his grandmother in his childhood, he can't help feeling warm.

- 情不自禁做某事 can't help doing sth. 
- 不得不做某事 can't help but do sth.
- 不能帮助做某事 can't help (to) do sth.

**4.** 为使这款产品在竞争激烈的市场中**脱颖而出**，公司负责人建议把传统文化融入到产品的设计理念中。（integrate）

> To make this product **stand out** in the fiercely competitive market, the person in charge of the company suggested integrating traditional culture into the design concept of this product.
