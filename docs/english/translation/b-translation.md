# B 字母翻译练习

**1.** 你计划在你的户头上定期存放多少钱？（basis）

> How much money do you plan to **deposit in** your bank account on a regular basis?

**2.** 他被之前的荣誉蒙蔽了，应该为自己的失败负责。（blame, blind）

> He was blinded **by** his past glory, so he should be blamed **for** his failure.

**3.** 我难以置信那名人气网红去年双十一卖出了 15000 支口红。（Barely）

> **Barely can I believe that** the popular online celebrity sold 15000 **lipsticks** on the previous Double Eleven Day.

**4.** 请记住，如果你对交规视而不见，将会被禁止驾车一年。（bear, ban）

> Please bear in mind that if you turn a blind eye to the traffic rules, you'll be banned from driving for a year.

**5.** 一群工程师为了确保公司在之后几年的正常运行，废寝忘食地努力工作。（band）

> A band of engineers are working hard, **neglecting sleep and meals** to ensure the normal **operation** of the company in the coming years.

**6.** 这位彬彬有礼的小提琴家计划代表剧院举行一次慈善音乐会，来为孤儿们筹钱。（benefit）

> This **well-mannered** violinist has planned to hold a **benefit** concert **on behalf of the theater** to raise money for the orphans.
>
> well-behaved 的意思是“表现好的、守规矩的”，通常用来夸奖小孩（乖巧的）或宠物。形容成年人“彬彬有礼”，应该用 well-mannered, polite, 或 courteous。

**7.** 为了阻止人才流失，有关当局千方百计为人才提供优惠政策。（brake）

> To **put the brakes on the loss of talent**, **the authorities concerned** are trying every possible means to provide talent with **preferential / favourable policies**.
>
> 当 "talent" 泛指“人才”这一群体概念时，通常是不可数名词。

**8.** 这位侦探不忘初心，多年来埋首于调查，终于揭露了真相。（bury）

> The detective **remained true to his original ambition / aspiration**, **buried himself in** the investigation for years and finally revealed the truth.

**9.** 你的父母会支持你的，哪怕你被卷入这场严重的冲突。毕竟，血浓于水。（back）

> Your parents will **back you up** even if you **are caught up in / involved in** the severe conflict. After all, blood is thicker than water.
>
> even though = 尽管（引导已知事实）
>
> even if = 即使 / 哪怕（引导假设 / 让步）

**10.** 当你遭遇精神崩溃时，深吸一口气并转移自己的注意力可以让你重新恢复生机。（bring）

> When you **suffer a mental breakdown**, taking a deep breath and **distracting yourself from it** can bring you back to life / restore your energy.

**11.** 我出生贫寒，当我没有辜负父母的期望考入复旦大学时，我情不自禁的大哭起来。（burst）

> **Born into a poor family**, I couldn't help bursting out crying when I **lived up to** my parents' expectation**s** and **was admitted into** Fudan University.

**12.** 在你被繁重的工作量累到精疲力尽之前，你最好在工作和休息之间找到平衡。（better）

> You'd better **strike / keep a balance between** work and rest before you'**re exhausted / worn out / tired out** by **a heavy workload**.

**13.** 我在浏览网站时，看到一个线上校园招聘服务在国内几大求职网站推出，这让我印象颇深。（browse）

> When I was browsing websites, I saw that an online campus hiring service was **available / rolled out / launched** **on** several major domestic job-hunting websites, which impressed me a lot.

**14.** 中国拥有富有朝气的年轻一代，他们愿意承担缩小中国和发达国家间距离的重任。（boast）

> China boasts a vibrant / vigorous generation, who **are** willing to bear the burden of narrowing the gap between China and developed countries.

**15.** 政府对捕猎重拳出击，这样可以将对环境的危害降到最少，而且可以重振当地的生物多样性。（blow）

> The government **struck a heavy blow against / at** poaching(偷猎), so that it could not only minimize the damage to the environment but also **revive / restore** the local biodiversity.

**16.** 区域和平与稳定遭受危害，正是这位硬核的反恐部门官员扭转了这一局面。（branch）

> It was the **tough / hardline** officer at the anti-terrorism branch who reversed the situation where regional peace and stability were **undermined**.

**17.** 新兴国家转向清除贸易壁垒，而不是将经济危机归咎于外部的市场条件。（blame）

> Emerging countries turned to removing the **trade barriers**, instead of blaming external market conditions for the economic crisis.

**18.** 基于总统与病人有过密切接触这一事实，总统将继续隔离，并采取推荐的预防措施。（base）

> Based on the fact that the president has **had close contact with** the patient, he'll **remain isolated** and adopt the recommended **preventive measures**.
>
> 在现代英语和许多实际应用中，Based on 已经被权威词典收录为复合介词（相当于 on the basis of），不再被视为悬垂修饰。

**19.** 白宫将公布大刀阔斧的方案与经济危机作斗争，包括为某些行业提供财政援助、减税和针对性救济。（battle）

> The White House will **reveal / release sweeping / bold proposals / plans** to battle against the financial crisis, including financial support, **tax cuts** and targeted relief for certain industries.
