# 暑假翻译60组

!!! note "1A"

    **1.** 由于污染严重，鱼的数量正在**逐渐**减少。（amount）
    
    > Due to serious pollution, the amount of fish is **gradually** decreasing / on the decrease / on the decline.
    
    **2.** 几年之前他成了一名志愿者，这对他的生活**产生了巨大的影响**。（difference）
    
    > He became a volunteer several years ago, which has **made all the difference** in his life.
    
    **3.** 我决定每月**留出**一些钱，这样明年暑假就可以去香港玩玩。（trip）
    
    > I have decided to **set aside** some money every month, so I can go on a trip to Hong Kong next summer vacation.
    
    **4.** 他把这张战火中**幸存下来**的画视若珍宝，小心翼翼地放在抽屉里。（regard）
    
    > He regarded the painting, which **survived the war**, as his most treasured possession / a treasure and put it in the drawer with great care.
    >
    > "视若珍宝" → as his most valuable property 中 property 偏"财产、不动产"，语感略硬。用 as a treasure / as his most treasured possession 会更传神。

!!! note "1B"

    **1.** 他一旦许下诺言，决不食言，而且努力实现。（Once）
    
    > Once he makes / has made a promise, he will never break it and will try to / strive to fulfill it.
    >
    > 主将从现。使用 has made 侧重于“一旦已经做出”。
    >
    > Once you start, you can't stop.（一旦开始就停不下来），零条件句，主句不用将来时。
    
    **2.** 绿色不仅象征生命，而且它常与环保联系在一起。（Not only）
    
    > Not only does green symbolize life, but it is also often associated with environmental protection.
    >
    > 当 Not only 置于句首修饰第一个分句时，该分句需要部分倒装：
    >
    > Not only + 助动词 / be 动词 / 情态动词 + 主语 + 动词原形/分词
    >
    > but also 后面不倒装。
    
    **3.** 这项职业工作时间长，劳动辛苦并充满困难，从而导致很多年轻人不愿**从事**。（fill）
    
    > This profession is filled with long hours, hard work and many difficulties, which leads many young people to be unwilling to **take it up**.
    >
    > take up 开始从事；占用（时间，空间）；接受（任务）；收起（衣物）
    >
    > profession 为需要高等教育 + 专业知识的职业，强调专业性、门槛。
    
    **4.** Tom 是个**名副其实**的好爸爸，为了孩子的身心健康，他常常每周花几小时和儿子一起玩游戏，看故事书。（sake）
    
    > Tom is a **truly** good father, as he often spends several hours playing games and reading storybooks with his son every week for the sake of his child's physical and mental health.

!!! note "2A"

    **1.** **众所周知**，要劝服一个**烟瘾**很重的人**戒烟**是很难的。（persuade）
    
    > **As we all know / As is known to all**, it is difficult to persuade **a heavy smoker** to **quit smoking** / into quitting smoking.
    >
    > **persuade sb. do to/into doing**
    
    **2.** 我们明天能否外出踏青得视天气情况而定。（depend on）
    
    > Whether we can go for a spring outing tomorrow depends on the weather.
    
    **3.** 不管怎么忙，你都不应该忽视健康，最好每天锻炼半小时。（neglect）
    
    > However / No matter how busy you are, you shouldn't neglect your health, and you'd better exercise for half an hour every day.
    
    **4.** 向**周围**那些**关心和爱护**我们的人表示感激，是我们每天生活中应该做**的事**，而不仅只在感恩节才做。（show）
    
    > Showing gratitude to / appreciation to those **around us** who **care about us and look after us** is **something** that we should do in our daily life, not just on Thanksgiving Day.

!!! note "2B"

    **1.** 毫无疑问，我们正在做的事有助于社会的**进步**。（doubt）
    
    > **There's** no doubt that what we're doing helps society **progress / move forward**.
    >
    > society 泛指社会 不加 the
    
    **2.** 对**他这个年纪的**男孩而言，他难以独自应付**眼前的**困难。（cope）
    
    > It's difficult for a boy **of his age** to cope with the difficulties **facing** him on his own.
    
    **3.** 尽管他的建议听起来挺好，但我认为在目前阶段既**不现实也行不通**。（Although）
    
    > Although his suggestion sounds nice, I don't think it's **practical or feasible** at present.
    >
    > practical 实际的、切实可行的，强调现实中可操作，不是空想 a practical solution / approach
    >
    > feasible 可行的、行得通的，强调条件/技术上能够做到 a feasible plan / proposal
    
    **4.** 现在很多年轻人学会独立自主，并自己创业谋生，不依靠父母，这是多么**令人欣慰**啊！（instead）
    
    > How **comforting** it is that many young people today have learnt to be independent and to start their own businesses to make a living instead of relying on **their** parents!

!!! note "3A"

    **1.** 让青少年远离毒品是每个人的职责。（responsibility）
    
    > It is everyone’s responsibility to keep adolescents / teenagers away from drugs.
    
    **2.** 这个国际学术会议的与会者们对志愿者**全面周到**的服务深表感激。（grateful）
    
    > The participants of the international academic conference were grateful to the volunteers for their **comprehensive and attentive** service.
    >
    > be grateful to sb. for sth.
    >
    > comprehensive 全面的；attentive 周到的
    >
    > services → service 作"服务"讲时，通常用作不可数名词。用 services（复数）指"具体服务项目"时成立

    **3.** 与呆在家里相比，这位充满活力的老人更喜欢**在阳光下**进行一些户外活动，以及和**社区**的老人们聊天。（prefer）

    > This energetic old man prefers having some outdoor activities **in the sun** and chatting with old people in the **community** to staying at home.
    
    **4.** 三年里，他在实验室**废寝忘食**地做科学研究，最终**取得了巨大成功**，但**别人赞扬他**时他总是保持**谦逊**的姿态。（remain）
    
    > He did / conducted scientific research in the lab for three years, **neglecting sleep and meals**, and eventually **made great achievements / achieved great success**, but he always remained **humble** when other people **paid him compliments / when other people praised him / when he was praised by others**.

!!! note "3B"

    **1.** 由于学业成绩优秀，他**被授予奖学金**。（award）
    
    > He **was awarded a scholarship** for his excellent academic performance.
    
    **2.** 你不觉得最好**考虑所有可能的后果**后再做出决定比较好吗？（before）
    
    > Don't you think it's better to **take all the possible consequences into consideration** before making the decision?

    **3.** 第一次和我父母**去度假**时，那里**无以伦比的**美景给我留下了深刻的印象。（The first time)

    > The first time I **went on holiday** with my parents, I was deeply impressed by the **unparalleled** scenery there.
    
    **4.** 最近几年，房价不断上涨，这让很多原本有购房打算的年轻人望而却步。(plan v.)
    
    > In recent years, the housing prices have been continuously rising, which has discouraged many young people who planned to purchase a house.
    >
    >“房价”作为一个宏观的市场现象时，英语习惯用复数；只有指代具体某一套房子的价格时，才用单数。

!!! note "4A"

    **1.** 这条高架道路（elevated road）是为**缓解**交通堵塞而设计的。（design）
    
    > The elevated road is designed to **relieve** traffic congestion / jams.
    
    **2.** 一项**新颁布**的法律规定任何人在**野生动物保护区**里狩猎都是违法的。（it 作形式宾语）
    
    > A **newly-issued** law made it illegal for anyone to hunt in **the wildlife reserves**.

    > Some businessmen's greedy **nature** and lack of social responsibility will **end up hurting** themselves.
    
    **4.** 坦率地说，我相信那些热爱山中清静环境的人们总能在这里找到**属于自己**的一方天地。（peace）
    
    > To be frank / Frankly speaking, for those who love **the peace of the mountains**, I believe that they can always find a place **that fits them** here.

!!! note "4B"

    **1.** 她毕业后**不久**，就去美国一所大学深造了。（after）
    
    > **Soon after** she graduated, she went to a university in America to further her studies.
    
    **2.** 如果你方便的话，帮我在面包房买些面包。（convenient）
    
    > **Pick up** some bread **at** the bakery if it's convenient **for** you.

    **3.** 即使小孩子很好奇、很调皮，你也不能**对他们太过严厉**。（severe）

    > Even if children are very curious and very naughty, you shouldn't **be too severe with them**.
    >
    > be severe with sb
    
    **4.** 就孩子该**报考**哪所大学，许多父母**不辞辛劳地**做很多调查，**咨询很多专家**。(pains)
    
    > Many parents **take pains to** do a lot of investigations and **inquire of / consult many specialists** about which university their children should **apply to**.
    >
    > consult sb. / inquire about sth. / inquire of sb.
    > 
    > apply for + 事物；apply to + 机构

!!! note "5A"

    **1.** **再**努力一下，你就能梦想成真。（effort）
    
    > Make **another** effort, and you'll realize your dream.
    
    **2.** 他们**从未想到**这么多机构同意赞助他们的慈善计划。（occur）
    
    > **It never occurred to them that** so many institutions / organizations agreed to sponsor their charity project.
    >
    > sponsor + sth. 不用 for

    **3.** 从某种程度上讲，当今快节奏的生活已经大大影响了生活的质量，所以有很多人想要搬到乡下去。（extent）

    > To some extent, the fast-paced life nowadays / of today has greatly **affected** / influenced the quality of life, so many people want to move to **the** countryside.
    >
    > affect 直接的、明显的、可见的影响；influence 间接的、潜移默化的、渐进的影响
    
    **4.** 这些小伙子们**有很多共同点**，比如都喜爱看漫画，喜爱上网，甚至还喜欢在冬天滑雪。（fond）

    **3.** 没有充足的睡眠，学生不可能集中精神听课，精力充沛地做功课。（attentively）

    > Without sufficient / adequate / enough sleep, it's impossible for students to listen to their teachers attentively and do their **schoolwork** energetically.

    > schoolwork 单数；attentively adv. 专心地
    
    **4.** 小孩子很容易**认同**卡通片中的人物，因此在很大程度上会受他们言行的影响。(identify)

!!! note "7A"

    **1.** 未经允许看私人信件是违法的。（without）

!!! note "9A"

    **1.** 昨天 Jane **请了病假**，今天我想帮她**补课**。（leave n.）
    
    > Jane **asked for sick leave** yesterday, and I want to help her **make up her missed lessons** today.
    
    **2.** 这段**30分钟的视频**传到网上只有两个小时，已有上于人浏览过。（post）
    
    > Only two hours after the **30-minute video** was posted on the Internet, thousands of people had viewed it.

    **3.** 只有**坚持**每天多听多读，你的英语成绩才能**在不久的将来**赶上你的同学。（Only）

    > Only by **persisting in** more listening and reading every day, will your English performance catch up with that of your classmates' **in the near future**.
    >
    > Only + 主语 → 不倒装；Only + 状语（by… / when… / then / here…）→ 倒装
    >
    > 同样会引起部分倒装的否定词 / 半否定词：
    >
    > | 句首词 | 例句 |
    > | --- | --- |
    > | Never | Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset. |
    > | Seldom / Rarely | Seldom does he go to the cinema. |
    > | Hardly… when… | Hardly had I arrived when the phone rang. |
    > | Not until | Not until midnight did he come home. |
    > | No sooner… than… | No sooner had I sat down than the doorbell rang. |
    > | Little | Little did he know about the plan. |
    
    **4.** 前天，我们学校庆祝了50年校庆，那是一个盛大的场合，很多名人和**校友**出席了庆祝活动。（when）
