# 翻译

!!! info "格式样例"
    以下内容为翻译练习的格式样例，仅供参考排版布局。

---

!!! note "第一组"

    **1.** 正是因为他的坚持不懈，他最终克服了所有困难。（perseverance）
    
    > It was **because of his perseverance** that he finally overcame all the difficulties.
    
    **2.** 随着人工智能的快速发展，许多传统行业正面临巨大的挑战。（rapid development）
    
    > With the **rapid development of artificial intelligence**, many traditional industries are facing enormous challenges.
    
    **3.** 只有通过不断练习，你才能提高你的英语口语水平。（continuous practice）
    
    > Only **through continuous practice** can you improve your spoken English.
    
    **4.** 他不但对科学研究做出了巨大贡献，而且还积极参与慈善事业。（make great contributions to）
    
    > Not only did he **make great contributions to** scientific research, but he also took an active part in charity work.

---

!!! note "第二组"

    **1.** 毫无疑问，保护环境是每个人的责任。（responsibility）
    
    > There is no doubt that protecting the environment is everyone's **responsibility**.
    
    **2.** 这本小说基于真实事件，深深打动了无数读者。（be based on）
    
    > This novel, **based on** real events, deeply touched countless readers.
    
    **3.** 我永远不会忘记我第一次踏上这所大学校园的那一天。（set foot on）
    
    > I will never forget the day when I first **set foot on** the campus of this university.
    
    **4.** 尽管这项任务极具挑战性，他们还是按时完成了。（extremely challenging）
    
    > Although the task was **extremely challenging**, they still completed it on time.
