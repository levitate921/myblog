# A 字母翻译练习

**1.** 不论何时，只要你想聊，我都在。（available）

> No matter when you want to chat, I'm available.

**2.** 国会有望在下次会议中通过有关防止污染的新法案。（adopt）

> The Congress is expected to adopt a new bill / act about anti-pollution at the next session.

- 国会 The Congress
- 有望 be expected to
- 通过法案 adopt a bill / an act
- 防止污染 anti-pollution
- 国会的会议用 session
- in / at a meeting 区别：侧重状态还是事件
- at a conference
- in session（正在运作）/ at a session
- in Congress（在国会中）/ at the congress

**3.** 法官正要宣判的时候，被告女演员的粉丝涌进了法庭。（about）

> The judge was about to deliver the verdict when the fans of the accused actress flooded into the court.

- 宣判 deliver the verdict
- 被告的 accused

**4.** 和平易近人的妹妹不同，她是个烦人精，装腔作势，看什么都不顺眼。（airs）

> Unlike her approachable sister, she's an annoyance, putting on airs and finding fault with everything.

- 平易近人的 approachable
- 烦人精 an annoyance
- 看什么都不顺眼 find fault with everything

**5.** 形势对我们有利，是时候提起上诉，抗议不公正的裁决。（advantage）

> The situation is to our advantage, so it's time we appealed against the unfair ruling.

- 对...有利 to sb.'s advantage
- 是时候做 it is high time we did sth.
- 上诉 appeal against
- 裁决 ruling

**6.** 他如此专注于疫苗研发，没有注意到外面已经下起瓢泼大雨。（So, absorb）

> So absorbed was he in developing the vaccine that he didn't notice it was raining cats and dogs outside.

- 下瓢泼大雨 rain cats and dogs

**7.** 正是求知欲驱使着她不断前进，离开舒适区，探索未知的科学领域。（appetite）

> It is the appetite for knowledge that drives her to advance continuously, get out of the comfort zone and explore the unknown scientific fields.

- ...欲 the appetite for sth.
- 驱使 drive
- 不断前进 advance continuously
- 离开舒适区 get out of the comfort zone

**8.** 一半的城市家庭缺乏室内饮用水，四分之一的家庭无法用到抽水马桶，不论是公共厕所还是私人卫生间。（access）

> Half of the urban families lack / are lacking in indoor drinking water, and one quarter of them have no access to toilets, either public or private.

- 缺少 lack sth. / be lacking in sth.；名词形式 a lack of sth.
- 无法用到 have no access to

**9.** 我们已派代表与供货商接洽，以便精益求精，更好地为顾客服务。（approach）

> We have sent a delegate / representative to approach the supplier in order to strive for perfection / excellence and serve the customers better.

- 代表 delegate / representative
- 代表团 delegation
- 接洽，交谈；对付，处理 approach
- 精益求精 strive for perfection / excellence

**10.** 距离我们家只有一箭之遥的建筑工地复工了，我别无选择，只能买个降噪耳机。（alternative）

> The building site within a stone's throw of our home returned to work, so I have no alternative but to buy noise-cancelling earphones.

- 一箭之遥 within a stone's throw
- 复工 sth. returned to work
- 降噪耳机 noise-cancelling earphones

**11.** 我不认可整形手术，美丽不过是外表，维持亲密关系的秘诀是互相尊重，换位思考。（approve）

> I don't approve of cosmetic surgery, for beauty is only skin-deep and the key to maintaining an intimate relationship is mutual respect and putting oneself in others' shoes.

- 批准 approve sth.
- 赞同；认可 approve of sth.
- 整容手术 cosmetic surgery
- 不过是外表 is only skin-deep
- 亲密的 intimate
- 互相尊重 mutual respect(n.)
- 换位思考 put oneself in others' shoes

**12.** 我承认自己现在只是一个小白，但如果我一直致力于枯燥的日常工作，有朝一日也能声名鹊起。（apply）

> I admit that I'm just a greenhand now, but if I always apply myself to boring routines, I can make a name / rise to fame someday.

- 小白 greenhand
- 致力于 apply oneself to
- 声名鹊起 make a name / rise to fame

**13.** 校长向家长们保证，学生在校可享受到健康均衡的饮食，没必要专程到校门口送饭。（assure）

> The headmaster / principal assured the parents that students could enjoy balanced and healthy diets at school, and there was no need to specially deliver meals to the school gate.

- 向...保证 assure sb. that / of sth.

**14.** 他嗅到了计划中的风险，但是不入虎穴，焉得虎子，他压下心中的紧张感，等待最佳时机的来临。（awake，await）

> He awoke to the risks in the plan, but nothing ventured, nothing gained, so containing his nervousness, he awaited the coming of the perfect chance.

- 意识到 awake to
- 不入虎穴焉得虎子 nothing ventured, nothing gained
- 压下紧张感 contain one's nervousness
- 等待 await = wait for

**15.** 经理安排我出席会议，并在会上发表演讲，因为我比其他人更有优势。（arrange，address，advantage）

> The manager arranged for me to attend the meeting and address it because I had an advantage over others.

- 安排 arrange for sb. to do sh.
- 在会上发表演讲 address the meeting（没有 address a speech）
- 比...有优势 have an advantage over sb.

**16.** 这位教授在语言学领域取得了卓越成就，因此受邀在国际会议上发言。他在会上呼吁各国学者加强跨文化合作。（address，appeal）

> The professor made outstanding achievements in the field of linguistics, so he was invited to address the international conference, at which he appealed to scholars from different countries to strengthen cross-cultural cooperation.

- 卓越成就 outstanding achievements
- 语言学 linguistics
- 呼吁 appeal to sb. to do sth.
- 学者 scholars

**17.** 世卫组织呼吁人们保持理智，不可以以地名命名疾病，或对中国和武汉污名化。（appeal）

The WHO appealed to people to stay rational, and not to name diseases after places or stigmatize China and Wuhan.

> 保持理智 stay rational
> 以地名命名 name sth. after places

**18.** 面对新冠疫情，日本政府曾雪中送炭，现在日本确诊节节攀升，山川异域，风月同天，中国绝不会袖手旁观。（aid）

> In the face of / Faced with / Facing the novel coronavirus, the Japanese government offered timely aid when we were in trouble, so now, with confirmed cases increasing continuously in Japan, China will not stand by because we're under the same sky though miles apart.

- 面对 in the face of / faced with / facing
- 雪中送炭 offer timely aid when sb. was in trouble
- 袖手旁观 stand by
- 山川异域，风月同天 we're under the same sky though miles apart

**19.** 到南非的第三个月，我已经适应了没有电器，没有自来水的生活，不再绞尽脑汁想要回到国内。（accustom）

> Three months after I came to South Africa, I was accustomed to the life there with no electrical appliances or tap water, and no longer racked my brains to get back to my country.

- 习惯于 be accustomed to
- 自来水 tap water
- 绞尽脑汁 rack one's brains

**20.** 这项新政和我们长久以来的理念不符，新政过分强调了个人利益，对弱势群体熟视无睹。（accord）

> The new policy is not in accord with our long-held beliefs, which lays too much emphasis on personal interests, turning a blind eye to the disadvantaged groups.

- 与...一致 be in accord / line / accordance with
- 弱势群体 the disadvantaged groups
