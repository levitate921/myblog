# 有提示词

## 谓语动词

考查**时态**（一般现在/过去/将来、现在/过去完成时等）、**语态**（主动/被动）、**主谓一致**。

## 非谓语动词

考查 to do（目的/将来/固定搭配）、doing（主动/进行/伴随/介词后）、done（被动/完成）。

- Over a million people ____(oppose) to Britain's exit from the European Union marched through central London to demand a second referendum.
  - 答案：**opposed**
  - 解析：主句谓语是 marched，空处作后置定语。**be opposed to...** 反对……，省略 who were；oppose 是及物动词，也可作 **opposing** 直接加宾语（opposing the plan），不加 to。
  
- The deepening Brexit crisis risked ____(sink) U.K. Prime Minister's premiership.
  - 答案：**sinking**
  - 解析：句意：不断加深的英国脱欧危机让英国首相的任期面临险境。risk 后接 doing：risk doing sth.（冒险/有风险做某事），固定搭配。**take the risk of doing**。

## 名词、形容词等

名词考察单复数，所有格；形容词考察比较级、最高级。

# 无提示词

## 并列连词

and, but, or, so, for, yet

## 名词性从句

that, what, who/whom/whose, which, when/where/why/how, whether/if

## 定语从句 

who/whom/whose, which, that, where, when, why, as

- Many cases have been reported in ____ aircrew have received their annual radiation limit
  over a single flight and accordingly developed chronic diseases.
  - 答案：**which**
  - 解析：已有许多病例报告"机组人员在一次飞行中就达到了年度辐射上限"，并因此患上慢性病。划线部分为定语从句，修饰 cases（这些病例中）；in which 中 in 是介词，关系词用 which（介词后不用 that）。
 
## 状语从句 

because, although/though, if, unless, when, while, since, until, so that

- ____ ____ headlines about the flu are gone, hospitals are emptied of flu patients.
  - 答案：**The moment**
  - 解析：The moment 作连词引导时间状语从句，一……就，相当于 as soon as。
  
## 介词

固定搭配、语境介词。

## 冠词

a, an, the

## 代词

- Leading scientists and public health officials have the capability to keep us much safer from flu.
  ____ need your quick and decisive support to succeed.
  - 答案：**They**
  - 解析：指代上文复数主语 scientists and public health officials，作后句主语。

## 助动词，情态动词

- do, does, did
- can, could, must, should, may, might
